# TuritoSchools project brief

Updated: 2026-09-21. This document records the agreed direction and the current review implementation.

## Audience and stage

- Launch market: United States.
- School and district buyers include superintendents, principals, teachers, curriculum leaders, counselors, and technology/privacy teams.
- Students and families are additional audiences; distinguish buyer-facing descriptions from student-facing instructions.
- There are no customers yet. Do not fabricate testimonials, customer logos, adoption statistics, or outcome evidence. Demonstrate the product and describe verified capabilities accurately.
- Prices will not be displayed for now.

## Agreed positioning

One connected teaching-and-learning ecosystem serving schools, teachers, and students. Explain the audience's needs first, then the solution/package that supports them.

Keep AI visible where informative. Do not remove it or repeat an AI slogan in every section. AI supports workflows; educators retain the decisions appropriate to their roles. Identify student-facing AI explicitly rather than implying a human tutor or counselor.

Demonstrate verified connections between preparation, learning, practice, assessment, and insights. Do not imply that the product handles every school operation, such as transportation or payroll. Do not assert integration or cross-package data flows without product confirmation.

## Agreed homepage hero wording

Eyebrow: **AI support for your school**

Headline: **One intelligent learning ecosystem.**

Second line: *Built for schools, teachers, and students.*

Description: **Help teachers prepare lessons, give students guided practice, and see where extra support is needed.**

The user prefers this wording. Do not reopen it without a reason or request. Static versus rotating presentation is not settled by approval of the wording.

## Four-package structure confirmed by the user

| Working solution name | Scope discussed |
| --- | --- |
| School & District Insights | Engagement, learning progress, and performance reporting. |
| Teacher Tools | Planning, material creation, assessments, and AI-assisted review. |
| Academic Support | Academic resources, guided practice, study tools, and the included Virtual AI Tutor. |
| College Readiness | Test preparation, profiles, college exploration, and the included Virtual AI Counselor. |

Academic Support and College Readiness are the two offerings under student support. Virtual AI Tutor and Virtual AI Counselor are included capabilities, not fifth and sixth packages. The tutor supports academic learning; the counselor supports college planning. Detailed feature availability and final public naming still require verification.

## Information architecture: recommendations, not approved implementation

- Audience-led navigation and clear solution descriptions are the proposed approach.
- Existing school and teacher pages can also function as their respective solution detail pages; avoid redundant copies.
- A separate pricing/packages comparison page is not currently necessary. Explain the four offerings on the homepage, with no prices.
- The assistant previously proposed a short For Students overview with separate Academic Support and College Readiness pages because the reviewed student page was very long.
- The user authorized the assistant to choose the clearest Student-page structure. The implemented approach keeps `/for-students` as a short overview and gives Academic Support and College Readiness separate detail pages. This avoids a very long combined page and gives each solution a direct URL.
- The tutor and counselor do not require independent pages at this stage.

## Editorial and workflow rules

- Use US terminology consistently: district where appropriate, grading, scores/points, review, class schedule, organized.
- Respect educators; avoid suggesting their school, teaching, or counseling is inadequate.
- Distinguish observed facts, proposed copy, open questions, and user-approved decisions.
- For each section: state its audience/purpose, current issue, proposed wording, reasoning, useful visual/interaction, and any claim needing verification.
- Use product evidence for product claims and current authoritative sources for external facts.
- Competitor scope must be verified before comparison; do not reduce competitors to one capability by assumption.
- The user authorized a site-wide content pass on `Review-Version`, with an original/current wording toggle on every public route. The current copy is implemented for review and has not been committed or deployed.
- The Student section now uses three routes: `/for-students` for the overview, `/for-students/academic-support`, and `/for-students/college-readiness`.
